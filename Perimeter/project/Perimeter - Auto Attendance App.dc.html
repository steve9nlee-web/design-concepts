PROTOTYPE / 01
Perimeter
Staff attendance that clocks itself. Company wi-fi plus a 100 m GPS fence, one minute of grace, every event stamped to a Google Sheet.
HOW A LOGIN IS TRUSTED
1
SSID matches CORP-STAFF
2
BSSID is on the site allow-list — a look-alike hotspot fails here
3
GPS fix falls inside the 100 m fence with accuracy under 30 m
4
All three hold for 15 s before the row is written
WHO MAY CHANGE THE RULES
The app lives on the staff phone with no accounts and no sign-in. Staff get three tabs — Status, Log, Sheet. Fence radius, grace period, the router allow-list and the Drive file details sit behind an admin PIN on the keypad chip; the Rules tab does not exist until the PIN is entered, and the session re-locks itself after {{ adminIdle }} s idle or on Lock.
THE PIN IS A GATE, NOT A GUARANTEE
A PIN checked on the handset stops ordinary staff — it does not stop a rooted phone or a repackaged APK. The server must own the settings and enforce them on every write, so a tampered device can change what it displays but never what gets recorded.
WHAT I CAN'T DO FROM HERE
This prototype can't write to your Drive folder — that needs a service account or an Apps Script bound to the sheet. The app screens are designed around that contract: the phone queues rows offline, a backend appends them, and the sync screen reports the result.
