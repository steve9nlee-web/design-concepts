PROTOTYPE / 01
Perimeter
Staff attendance that clocks itself. Company wi-fi plus a 100 m GPS fence, one minute of grace, every event stamped to a Google Sheet.
HOW A LOGIN IS TRUSTED
1
SSID matches CORP-STAFF
2
BSSID is on the site allow-list — a look-alike hotspot fails here
3
GPS fix falls inside the 100 m fence with accuracy under 30 m
4
All three hold for 15 s before the row is written
WHAT I CAN'T DO FROM HERE
This prototype can't write to your Drive folder — that needs a service account or an Apps Script bound to the sheet. The app screens are designed around that contract: the phone queues rows offline, a backend appends them, and the sync screen reports the result.
OPEN DECISIONS
Multi-site staff · who may edit a written row · retention period · whether managers see live presence or day totals only.
